# Fine Print visual direction

## Surface

Operate: a desktop investigation desk for comparing policy versions, submitting a claim, checking a challenge and following a finalized payout.

## Concept

Build the page as an evidence chronology. The main reading surface is a redline case file: the archived baseline and later Wayback capture sit side by side, the submitted passage is marked in red, and validator verdicts appear as brief marginal notes. The ledger reads like a public docket, not a marketing dashboard. The investigative redline direction and policy comparison are pinned by the build brief.

## System

- Use warm paper for reading areas, dark ink for primary text, and restrained vermilion for changed language and unresolved review states.
- Use Barlow Condensed for case headings and DM Sans for navigation and body copy. Reserve a monospace face for hashes, timestamps and transaction identifiers.
- Use thin rules, numbered clauses and underlined evidence links to evoke annotated legal text. Keep controls quiet and legible.
- Never put illustrative records in the confirmed ledger. Candidate evidence stays visibly unverified until it has live finalized proof.
- Desktop is the required viewport for this deliverable.
